What counts as what
Evidence
Independent of the model. The two readings below are this point measured at 10 m, which is the resolution the labels are being collected for; the existing land cover maps are at the foot, because they are other people's models and they fail in their own ways — which is the only reason they are worth a look.
Spectral profile
Median reflectance against wavelength, one line per year. A cropped field and rough grazing look the same in a 10 m RGB chip and separate in SWIR. This boundary is the one the ledger says caps change-F1.
Index over time
Nine annual composites, and — with dense on — every clear-sky observation behind them. The shape inside the year is the reading: a cropped field is bare, then green, then cut; rough grazing is flat.
What the existing land cover maps say
Dynamic World, WorldCover, ESRI, GLanCE and Hansen, at this point. Read them after you have made your own call, not before: they disagree with each other, they are coarser than the label being collected, and every one of them shows its end year for a reason.
